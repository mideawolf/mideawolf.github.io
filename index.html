Understanding Nonwoven Fabrics

The Material of the Future

Nonwoven fabrics are engineered materials created by bonding or interlocking fibers together using mechanical, thermal, or chemical processes. Unlike traditional textiles, they are neither woven nor knitted, allowing for highly specialized performance characteristics.

  • Versatile Applications: The primary material for premium dry and wet wipes, medical hygiene products, and industrial filtering.
  • Customizable Performance: Can be engineered for specific levels of absorbency, liquid repellency, resilience, softness, and strength.
  • Cost-Effective & Efficient: Advanced manufacturing ensures high output with consistent quality, suitable for both single-use and durable goods.
